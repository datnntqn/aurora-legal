Aurora

From Wikipedia, the free encyclopedia

An aurora is a natural light display in Earth's upper atmosphere caused by charged particles from the Sun colliding with atoms in the atmosphere.

These collisions excite oxygen and nitrogen, which emit light of different colors: green, red, and purple.

In the Arctic they're called northern lights; in the Antarctic, southern lights.
