Questions.

Is there a Mac version?

A Mac version is in active development — coming soon. It brings Mac-only powers like changing the web font & size on any site, alongside the full theming engine.

What's the refund policy?

Standard Apple App Store policy. The 3-day free trial needs no payment info, so you can try before you buy.

Does Aurora collect any data?

Only anonymous product-usage stats via TelemetryDeck — no personal data, no tracking, and your browsing never leaves your device. See the Privacy Policy.

How is it different from Noir or Dark Reader?

Aurora is built specifically for iOS — Focus Filter, Control Center, Live Activity, Auto Schedule, Siri/Shortcuts — and soon, Mac web-fonts. Stable on iOS 18+.
