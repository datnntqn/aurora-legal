Aurora

From Wikipedia, the free encyclopedia

An aurora is a natural light display in Earth's upper atmosphere caused by charged particles from the Sun colliding with atoms in the atmosphere.

These collisions excite oxygen and nitrogen, which emit light of different colors: green, red, and purple.

In the Arctic they're called northern lights; in the Antarctic, southern lights.

FAQ

Questions, answered.

Does Aurora collect any data?

No. Zero. Everything is stored locally on your device. No analytics, no tracking, no server. See the full Privacy Policy.

How is Aurora different from Noir or Dark Reader?

Aurora is the only Safari dark mode built specifically for iOS — with Focus Filter, Control Center widget, Live Activity, Auto Schedule, and Siri/Shortcuts integration. Tested and stable on iOS 18+.

Will it slow down Safari?

No. Aurora injects CSS at document_start for instant theming — no white flash. Site-specific optimizations for YouTube, Google, Reddit, Amazon, Wikipedia, Twitter, and more.

Does it work with other extensions like Grammarly or 1Password?

Yes. Aurora is designed to coexist with other Safari extensions.

What's the refund policy?

Standard Apple App Store refund policy. The 3-day free trial requires no payment info, so you can try before you buy.

Is there a Mac version?

Currently iOS and iPadOS only. Mac support is being evaluated.
